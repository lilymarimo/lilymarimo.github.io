Summer nights are too short. My eyes are tired, but my brain keeps working. So I close my eyes, wishing the thunderstorms to stop.<br> 
<br>
Rainy summer nights bring thunderstorms. The melody of calamity is beautiful; quick, sparkling, intense. So I fell asleep.<br> 
<br>
Perhaps it is the thunderstorms turning to melodies that I can listen. Perhaps it is the soul that found its resting place. Perhaps it is the morning, the ray of sunshine, the nights turning to long summer days.<br> 
<br>
But isn't God that kind, for creating nights and days.
