It is almost 9 pm. I am in the student's room, as usual, staring at myscreen with tens of tabs and Claude, analyzing this seemingly complicated data. Perhaps I took PhD because I wanted to have more drama in my life. The data certainly are being dramatic. Why would one minute synthesis difference flop the performance? Sure, impurities are the culprit. But oh come on, it is one fluffing minute!!! Now this reminds me that I was easily forgotten in a month, haha. Whoops, we're going off track.<br>
<br>
Right, the tabs on my brain, on my computer, on my laptop. Ah!!! The tab that haunts me with a voice of, "A PhD? With that research? Are you kidding me?". I hate that voice. And then another voice, "A PhD? In the third year, your seniors already published in Nature! They have three papers already at this point. A PhD? You are seriously kidding me". Ah those fluffers. I would then remember the little kid in the corner of the room, far from those fluffers, who studies very hard, who presents in a bunch of conferences, who bought a huge whiteboard and filled it with pinning analysis from the 70s and 80s. I remember the person who went to the lab at 10 am and return at 12 am, the one who repeatedly cried on the floor in front of the glovebox at 11 pm, the one who caught the 11 pm train from Yokohama to Tokyo, the one who went to Jeju and cried herself to sleep. The one who was blessed with parents who fully support her education, the one who came back to her room to cry on the floor because she cut her safety net off, the one who handled numerous rejections, the one who says, "Fluff you fluffers! I got this, I will defend this!".<br>
<br>
I am sorry dear, there is no letter for you today. I still love you though, walk faster love! See you someday sweetheart. XOXO.
