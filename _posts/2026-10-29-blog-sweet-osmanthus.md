I am waiting for my simulation to finish. It is 23:41 and I am still in the lab. It has been very cold lately, the temperature drops to 10 degrees today. I am still smiling every day, when I remember your flustered face, or when you said "taihen", or your performance in undergrad. I have been asking God to make this feeling softer, to turn it into something sincere that doesn't ask for certainty. I asked God for you to be happy, that you find ease in your daily life, that perhaps if there will ever be us in the future, I will love you beyond what this world offers, until the hereafter.<br>
<br>
I like you, I do not even know you. Well, except from your performance haha, or that late afternoon at the coffee shop. I have been drinking coffee, well, I try to have a cup a week. I allow myself to drink coffee again. I think I will buy decaf tomorrow. And probably I will bake a pumpkin cheesecake in the morning. My supervisor goes on a business trip so I don't need to go to the lab tomorrow. I kind of miss you, and I am ashamed of this feeling. I mean, well, think about it, we never met once even though our paths might have crossed so many times in the past. You might see me randomly through our mutual friends' updates. And yet, never once, we ever crossed paths back then. Life is funny.<br>
<br>
Will I like you if we met back then? Perhaps I will. I don't know. But perhaps I won't haha. My type was tech nerd back then. And you are not the type, as it seems like. Perhaps I won't be as mature as I am now as well. So, I think, I don't want to meet you in the past.<br>
<br>
When I went to Kyoto that day, I was walking in Kyoto gyoen, photographing trees. I also found this sweet osmanthus that bloomed beautifully. I like that I saw the flower that day, two days before I met you. I wonder, if we ever meet again, what kind of flower I will see.<br>
<br>
I hope you are doing well. But also, I hope that at least, your day is not as peaceful as it should be, that I would sometimes appear in your head. Just like the way you appear in mine. I hope that, for a split second, you remember me every day. I hope that it is not a selfish wish, hehe. I wish God will let our path crosses again in the future. Well, see you then ;)
