It was probably my first decaf coffee I had this year. I haven't had one since forever, although I know I like the taste. It paired very well with the pumpkin cheesecake. It was the only hot beverage I could order from the menu. I guess God lets you see a part of me when I spoiled myself.<br>
<br>
You chose to sat far from my chair. You looked like your mind went blank when we caught each other's eyes for the first time. I wish I could see myself, since I was as flustered as you were. You didn't talk to me much, you talked with our friend the whole time. But I guess, I was just relieved that you didn't. That way, I could see how your face lit up when you are excited about something, or when you laugh without holding back.<br>
<br>
It was brief, but I am glad that it happened. Our friend left us two in the room. And again, I was too shy to speak my mind out loud. Then you smiled when you said I must have been tired. I wish I could freeze the moment in my mind indefinitely.<br>
<br>
And now, you have invaded my routines. You are in my conference, my data analysis, my experiment, my smile, the sky, the stars, the hopes I utter to God. But this time, I know better, I will not beg to God, I will keep this feelings and let God does His magic. His magic of meeting us together in the first place. And most certainly, I do not care whether you reciprocate the feelings or not. I care that I like you, and I like you a lot.<br>
<br>
And until then, I will let you stay in my prayers. And I will wait for your visit to Tokyo, whenever that will be, it might be in this life, or the next one. See you again, and next time, I would rather walk through the buzzing streets of Tokyo with you than sipping my coffee nervously in front of you.
